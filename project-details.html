Project Overview

Al Azbakeya Park stands as one of Cairo's oldest and most historically significant gardens — a place closely connected to the architectural development of Khedivial Cairo. Within the park's revitalization, its heritage pergolas were recreated to bring back a defining element of the park's historical character.

Our team was entrusted with the manufacturing and execution of the heritage pergolas as a subcontractor to the main contractor, The Arab Contractors (Osman Ahmed Osman & Co.). The work began with studying historical references, then translating them into detailed shop drawings for each pergola design, and finally fabricating every component in our own wood facility before installing and finishing them on site.
