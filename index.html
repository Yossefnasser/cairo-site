About Us

Cairo International for Contracting & Architecture is a leading multidisciplinary company dedicated to delivering exceptional architecture, contracting, and specialized manufacturing solutions across Egypt and the Middle East region.

Our expertise spans across multiple disciplines including architectural design, general contracting, wood manufacturing, curtain wall systems, facade engineering, and comprehensive commercial project development. We combine technical excellence with creative innovation to create sustainable, functional, and aesthetically pleasing built environments.

With years of experience in the construction industry, we have established ourselves as a trusted partner for both public and private sector projects, consistently delivering on time and within budget while maintaining the highest standards of quality and safety.
