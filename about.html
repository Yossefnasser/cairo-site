About Cairo International

Building Ideas Into Reality

Cairo International for Decoration and Construction is an Egyptian company providing architecture, contracting, manufacturing, and execution services — carrying projects from the first drawing to the finished building.

Who We Are

Cairo International for Decoration and Construction is an Egyptian construction and architecture company based in Cairo. Our work spans architectural design, general contracting, curtain walls and facades, interior fit-out, and architectural woodwork manufactured in our own facility.

That combination defines how we operate. Because design, engineering, and production sit under one roof, details are resolved early, materials are controlled at the source, and what is drawn is what gets built.

  • Architecture & engineering under one roof
  • In-house wood manufacturing
  • Execution by our own teams
