The Walkway at SAADA

On Egypt's North Coast, Horizon Egypt Developments developed SAADA — a coastal destination where outdoor spaces are expected to withstand sun, salt air, and water exposure without losing their character. At the heart of its outdoor landscape, the project required a wooden walkway built for that environment.

Cairo International for Decoration and Construction delivered the walkway with its own professional team of engineers, craftsmen, and specialists — taking the work from the initial concept and design, through production, to installation and the final result on site.

Projects like this are the result of careful planning, attention to detail, quality materials, and a team that takes pride in every stage of the work.

02

Teak Wood — A Material for the Coast

One of the key materials selected for the walkway was teak wood (خشب التك) — known for its strength, durability, and natural resistance to moisture and water exposure, which makes it a strong choice for an outdoor project on the coast.

Working with teak, however, comes with its own challenges. The production and machining process requires special care, particularly because of the strong smell and fine wood dust generated during cutting and processing. Our craftsmen work with appropriate protective masks and safety precautions while carefully machining the material to achieve the required finish and dimensions.
